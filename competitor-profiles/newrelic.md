## Pricing

| Model | Description | Key Risk |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Free | 100GB/month ingest, 1 full-platform user | Solo developers only |
| Per-user | ~$99/user/month (full platform) | Expensive per-seat at scale |
| CCU (Compute Capacity Units) | Usage-based compute | Completely opaque — no public pricing |
| Data ingest | ~$0.30/GB (over free tier) | Traffic spikes inflate costs |

**Notable**: New Relic has changed pricing models 3+ times (per-server → per-seat → hybrid → consumption). This churn is a major trust issue.
**Hidden costs**: $418.80/user/month for top-tier (CostBench 2026). Teams track costs in spreadsheets.
**Risk**: CCU pricing is not public — must talk to sales to learn costs.
