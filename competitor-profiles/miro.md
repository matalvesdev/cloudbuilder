## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Key Limitations |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Free | $0 | Only 3 editable boards; rest become view-only |
| Starter | $10/user/month | Unlimited boards, guests cannot edit, no SSO |
| Business | $20/user/month | SSO, Jira integration, Azure DevOps |
| Enterprise | Custom | SCIM, audit logs, dedicated support |

**Billing traps** (major issue — Trustpilot 1.9/5):
- **Automatic guest billing**: Sharing a view-only link can trigger paid licenses. Users report being charged $20/person for viewers.
- **No guest/editor distinction at lower tiers**: Inviting 25 workshop participants = $2,674 annual charge
- **61% license waste**: Zylo February 2026 found 61% of Miro licenses go unused ($52K waste on $85K spend)
- **Feature lock**: Previously free features now gated behind Enterprise Advanced
- **Phone support**: Only for Enterprise

## Customer Sentiment (Reddit + TrustRadius + G2)

**Common praise**:
- Best-in-class visual collaboration
- Real-time co-editing works well
- Massive template library
- Great for workshops and brainstorming

**Common complaints**:
- **Billing practices are predatory**: "Most complained-about issue on Trustpilot." Auto-billing for viewers, no refunds, difficult cancellation
- **3-board free limit**: Oldest boards auto-lock to view-only — feels like a trap
- **Performance**: Large boards become nearly unusable
- **Customer support**: AI chatbot is "completely useless"; formal support is slow or non-responsive
- **Missing creative features**: Limited typography, path editing, design control
- **Figma competition**: Designers preferring Figma's board for deeper design capabilities
